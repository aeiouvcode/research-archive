# KEEP THE LIGHT

- Repository: https://github.com/aeiouvcode/keep-the-light
- Live: https://aeiouvcode.github.io/keep-the-light/

## Inspiration

The Antikythera dive-game Claude artifact posted by the official Three.js account, sent by the owner 2026-09-23 and green-lit ("Make", "Build it, using a game engine"). Original concept in the same shape: climb the stair, gather five lens panes, relight the lamp before the oil runs out.

## References

- The Three.js account's Antikythera dive-game post (X, 2026-09-23 13:25). Exact URL in chat history.

## Findings

- Live since 2026-09-23 15:04; iterated overnight through cycles c2-c31: storm and gusts that push you, foghorn, oil droplets, mute chip, run stats.
- Doubles as the proof-of-format for a Slapjam-sized game.

## Technical decisions

- One-file lighthouse climb game, Godot 4.3, all art and audio generated in code. Plays in browser and on phone.
