# O Empire

- Repository: https://github.com/aeiouvcode/o-empire-reborn
- Live: https://aeiouvcode.github.io/o-empire-reborn/

## Inspiration

The Steam game O EMPIRE! WARD OFF THY ROT. Built as an original atmospheric survival procession inspired by it, not a copy.

## References

- The developer's key art: misregistered halftone plates, a gray tower, a red flower field. It was used as the primary art direction, not as a mood reference.
- The developer's gameplay video: held low-frame-rate animation and an elevated oblique camera. Viewing from above changes how the game reads.

## Findings

- Side-by-side comparison of the red flower snow field scene: judged a close second.
- Pacing target is about an 11-minute walk. A careful run finishes in about 10:35 with all 8 caches.

## Technical decisions

- Canvas rendering in a single file.
- Ash and charcoal palette with crimson as the only saturated color.

## Gaps

- The original prototype builds were lost when their temporary hosts expired (2026-09-17). Their notes are in chat history only.
