## Findings

- Owner art feedback on KEIRYU: "A bit too swampy", "lovecraftian horror", "this gives the vibe of shrek's homw". Regraded brighter and colder: mountain stream, meadow banks, icy sky.

## Technical decisions

- Web track (KEIRYU, three.js): caustic water over pebbles, verlet line and float, 8 original Japanese stream species, tension fight, catch cards.
- Godot track (SUIKEI, own repo): gyotaku catch cards, journal. Dual-track per the Godot fleet rule.
