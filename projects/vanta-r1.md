# VANTA R1

- No repository - dossier and media delivered as documents
- Status: completed

## Inspiration

Owner brief (2026-09-16): "Design a running shoe which competes and beats the flagship on quality and price."

## References

- Nike Alphafly 3: the benchmark flagship for the whole design.

## Findings

- Delivered: concept dossier with renders and costing ($53 FOB, $89 variable/pair at scale, $180 retail vs $295), a 7-page manufacturing playbook (named suppliers, 9-step process, QC, 52-week calendar), a 40s showcase film with original score, an 8-slide keynote deck, and a claims audit plus narrated explainer video.
- Claims audit verdict: "promising, not proven". 202 g and $180 are targets; the 39.5 mm heel sits 0.5 mm under the World Athletics ceiling; PEBA/forked-plate benefits are literature-backed at category level, not for this shoe.
- Paper design only. The next real step would be a prototype and lab/road testing, not commissioned.
- The owner rejected the first film's audio as "somebody piercing my eardrums"; a softer remix shipped.

## Technical decisions

- Benchmark-first design: every claim costed and audited against the Alphafly 3 rather than asserted.
