# Afterimage Index

- Repository: https://github.com/aeiouvcode/afterimage-index
- Live: https://aeiouvcode.github.io/afterimage-index/

## Inspiration

Twelve original ink-washed character studies (Ren, Mika, Sora, Aki and others) in an interactive filmstrip. The originating brief is not recorded in the repo; it sits in the ISSEN orbit of ink-work builds.

## References

_None recorded yet._

## Findings

- Presented as numbered plates: step through one at a time, open the full index, or let the sequence run on its own. Marks left on plates stay on the device.

## Technical decisions

- A single self-contained index.html with the plates embedded. No network requests.
