# Convenience SaaS research

- No repository - research deliverables only
- Status: research delivered, no build, owner's hesitation unresolved

## Inspiration

The owner asked for convenience-software SaaS ideas, then issued a long brief demanding the full commercial case: problem, who pays, competitors, marketing, scale, funding, moat, survival, the "why would I switch" objection. He framed the working split himself: "Can this be fully autonomous-> no, i will lead, but you implement."

## References

- 22 sources cited in the five-idea playbook (2026-09).

## Findings

- Three documents delivered: a 13-page five-idea playbook (22 sources), an 8-page originality strategy, and a 4-page risk analysis.
- Narrowed fast to one idea: WhatsApp order assurance for distributors.
- The recommended gate is a paid 14-day shadow audit on 200 historic distributor orders, then a 90-day pilot - kill unless zero high-severity false posts and 5 paying design partners.
- The owner pulled back mid-thread: "the more i research, i see this is getting out of my experience little by bit". He asked instead about low-competition "boring money" lanes: recurring SMB services, implementation partner work, selling shovels into WhatsApp commerce. That hesitation is live and unresolved.

## Technical decisions

- Gate-first recommendation: evidence (shadow audit, paying design partners) before any build.

## Gaps

- No commitment from the owner; research parked.
